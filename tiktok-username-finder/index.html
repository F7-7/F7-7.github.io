    How checking works

    1. Open the profile. The Open button loads tiktok.com/@name in one reusable tab. If a profile shows up, mark it Taken.
    2. “Couldn't find this account” doesn't mean it's free. Banned, deleted and reserved names show the same page. Mark it No profile found.
    3. Confirm in the app. In TikTok go to Profile → Edit profile → Username and type the name. If TikTok accepts it, mark it Confirmed available. Only this step proves a name is free.

    Check at a human pace. Fast lookups get rate-limited. This tool never asks for your TikTok password, cookies or tokens, and makes no requests to TikTok itself. Your results, favorites and statuses stay in this browser. TikTok lets you change your username once every 30 days. Not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.
